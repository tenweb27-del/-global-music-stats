⚙️ Methodology — Real-Time Data Analytics Engine & Algorithmic Chart Generator

Our charts are not human-curated. They are automated, unbribable, and transparent.

How Verified Points Are Aggregated:
1. Audio Streaming Links (40% weight): Spotify, Apple Music, Boomplay, Audiomack, YouTube Music — verified via API, anti-bot filtered
2. Video Links (20%): YouTube, TikTok, Reels views + completion rate
3. Social Media Influencer Buzz (15%): Mentions from vetted influencers (10k+ followers) tracked via listening engine
4. Local Radio Airplay (15%): Data from Ghana/Nigeria/UK/US radio monitors
5. Vetted Independent Submissions (10%): Arena upvotes, Fan Base votes, Eloquence scores, editorial QA

Formula: Total Points = (Streams x 0.4) + (Video x 0.2) + (Buzz x 0.15) + (Radio x 0.15) + (Submissions x 0.1)
Deduplication, fraud detection, and 12-month archival vault ensure historical integrity.
